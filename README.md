# The Unwritten Company

**Every adventure becomes a legend. Most legends are accidents.**

An original tactical fantasy roguelike for iPhone, in which a custom hero and three recruited companions fight through coherently assembled, choice-driven miniature campaigns.

## Current verified development stage: v0.09 — Gate A review candidate

The game now has one **finishable replayable tactical skirmish** with two enemy types and four illustrated controllable prototype company members. The v0.09 art pass adds six new hand-authored canvas miniature designs, richer illustrated terrain and gilded frame detail, tactile portrait/command-rail materials, short terrain-clipped attack/critical/miss/Ward/movement/enemy-turn VFX, accessible text descriptions, reduced-motion handling, and drag-safe iPhone targeting. Training mode, deterministic enemy AI, legal paths, real AP/MP and HP, victory/defeat, original portrait assets and offline PWA are retained.
