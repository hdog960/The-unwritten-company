# The Unwritten Company

**Every adventure becomes a legend. Most legends are accidents.**

An original tactical fantasy roguelike for iPhone, in which a custom hero and three recruited companions fight through coherently assembled, choice-driven miniature campaigns.

## Current verified development stage: v0.08

The repository now includes an **actually finishable tactical skirmish**, not a playable campaign: four illustrative party adventurers, seeded attack rolls, pathfinding and movement, AP and movement budgets, and two original foes that choose targets, move, telegraph their intentions, attack, and can injure or incapacitate the party. The fight ends in victory when both foes are defeated, or defeat if the company falls, with an explicit replay action. A demonstration-only training table remains accessible separately.

The **v0.08 skirmish passed 65 Node tests, complete winning Chromium playthroughs at three iPhone-landscape viewport sizes and an offline enemy-turn test**. Real iPhone Safari, installed Home Screen lifecycle, public URL HTTP reachability, permanent battle saves and user art acceptance are **not yet verified**.
