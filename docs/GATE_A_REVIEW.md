# The Unwritten Company — Gate A actual-iPhone review

**Review target:** v0.09, The Illuminated Table. **State:** Awaiting the player's explicit visual AND control approval. v0.10 is blocked. Only this milestone and corrective art/UX fixes may proceed before approval.

**Open on iPhone Safari:** https://hdog960.github.io/The-unwritten-company/

## Test 1 — See the art in motion
Open the game online, rotate your iPhone to landscape, tap **Begin Skirmish**. Look at the four sculpted party members and the two enemy types. Are they **distinctive, premium and original** at your actual phone size? Look for brass frame/detail, terrain/tile quality, miniature textures, character identities, HP and target legibility. Look closely at the connected moss/river seams, geometric hero base emblems, six hand-finished figures, engraved book-table corners and four flat Inkfall floor symbols. The latest gentle watercolor transitions should not look like arrows or add distracting visual clutter. They must not look like blocking obstacles. Check that no miniature disappears at grid edges or overlaps another object unnaturally. This revision also adds ivy, water glints, gilded glyphs, rubble engravings and warm table lighting; judge whether those details make the battlefield feel premium rather than overly busy.

## Test 2 — Two-thumb comfort
Confirm that the four portraits on the left and Strike/Spark/Ward/Sigil, Move, Confirm/Cancel and End Turn on the right are all easily reachable **without covering the center map**, in both landscape rotations. Try taps near the very edges, including notch and Home gesture areas. Press a skill and inspect a far-away illegal target, then a legal target; hit Cancel and confirm no AP is spent. **New:** In a live skirmish only truly legal enemies should show strong brass targeting corners. Ward uses mint self-targeting, and only training permits faint marks on empty practice tiles. Watch a moved miniature follow the actual route rather than teleporting. The right-rail End Turn label should fit on one line. Drag your thumb across the battlefield: it must not accidentally select or attack. Move an ally around rubble and water. Test selecting each party member, including low-health heroes. **New:** with no ability armed, tap a hero directly on the battlefield to switch to them, and tap Briar or Shade to inspect HP and Guard; neither tap may spend AP. Look for visible threatened-party frames, a clear orange **!** portrait badge on heroes targeted by enemies, striped low-HP tracks, and a distinct **◇** badge when Ward-protected. Neither badge may block a thumb tap or hide the hero's name. Make sure opening instructions fit without scrolling.

## Test 3 — Animated combat
Attack an enemy, watching for readable short hit/miss/critical world effects; use Ward to see its distinct shield rune. End turns to observe orange hostile intent markings and enemy attacks. **New:** triangular exclamation markers mean incoming attacks; chevrons mean approaching enemies. The right rail also uses `!` versus `→` for these states, independent of color. Watch for a missed attack that does not shove an untouched target and a critical hit with stronger stagger. In this revision, the attacker should visibly prepare first, the defender must react only when the projectile/impact arrives, and second-enemy animations should not all start at the same instant. Check that target warnings are clear, HP changes are apparent, animations are fluid and do not conceal the battlefield, and text/controls never overlap or clip. Watch for distinct attack preparation, Spark projectile, Sigil glyph, Ward shield and movement along the previewed path; verify that characters react and the art still reads at real iPhone size. Inspect a victory or defeat and the Replay Skirmish button.

## Test 4 — Offline / Home Screen
After opening online once, use Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen. Try opening while offline, confirm the cached interface and skirmish function, then return online. **Warning:** this is an unsaved skirmish prototype; a page reload or background eviction may reset the battle. There is no cloud/local battle save or final campaign yet. Confirm basic install usability only, not persistent progress.
