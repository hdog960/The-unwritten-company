## Test 1 — See the art in motion
Open the game online, rotate your iPhone to landscape, tap **Begin Skirmish**. Look at the four sculpted party members and the two enemy types. Are they **distinctive, premium and original** at your actual phone size? Look for brass frame/detail, terrain/tile quality, miniature textures, character identities, HP and target legibility. Look closely at the connected moss/river seams, geometric hero base emblems, six hand-finished figures, engraved book-table corners and four flat Inkfall floor symbols. The gentle watercolor transitions should not look like arrows or add distracting visual clutter. The latest additional lantern and water glazes should improve depth without washing out target markers. Every miniature's new enamel base-light and soft backlight must stay within its own square. They must not look like blocking obstacles. Check that no miniature disappears at grid edges or overlaps another object unnaturally. This revision also adds ivy, water glints, gilded glyphs, rubble engravings and warm table lighting; judge whether those details make the battlefield feel premium rather than overly busy.

## Test 2 — Two-thumb comfort
Confirm that the four portraits on the left and Strike/Spark/Ward/Sigil, Move, Confirm/Cancel and End Turn on the right are all easily reachable **without covering the center map**, in both landscape rotations. Try taps near the very edges, including notch and Home gesture areas. Press a skill and inspect a far-away illegal target, then a legal target; hit Cancel and confirm no AP is spent. **New:** In a live skirmish only truly legal enemies should show strong brass targeting corners. Ward uses mint self-targeting, and only training permits faint marks on empty practice tiles. Watch a moved miniature follow the actual route rather than teleporting. **New:** after choosing an ability and tapping a target, inspect the curved ink-quill path and tiny target bookplate *under* the miniature. Invalid targets must have a broken coral route and cannot enable Confirm. A self-targeted Ward must show a stamped shield instead of a false projectile. The right-rail End Turn label should fit on one line. Drag your thumb across the battlefield: it must not accidentally select or attack. Move an ally around rubble and water. Test selecting each party member, including low-health heroes. **New:** with no ability armed, tap a hero directly on the battlefield to switch to them, and tap Briar or Shade to inspect HP and Guard; neither tap may spend AP. Look for visible threatened-party frames, a clear orange **!** portrait badge on heroes targeted by enemies, striped low-HP tracks, and a distinct **◇** badge when Ward-protected. On the battlefield, critically hurt heroes should have subtle scored enamel, Ward heroes an engraved shield, and heroes who ended their turn a muted diagonal hatch without covering their artwork. Neither badge may block a thumb tap or hide the hero's name. Make sure opening instructions fit without scrolling.

## Test 3 — Animated combat
Attack an enemy, watching for readable short hit/miss/critical world effects; use Ward to see its distinct shield rune. End turns to observe orange hostile intent markings and enemy attacks. **New:** triangular exclamation markers mean incoming attacks; chevrons mean approaching enemies. The right rail writes **Briar→Kess!** to mean Briar threatens Kess; `!` denotes an imminent attack, while a plain arrow denotes approach. Both remain distinguishable without relying on color. Watch for a missed attack that does not shove an untouched target and a critical hit with stronger stagger. In this revision, the attacker should visibly prepare first, the defender must react only when the projectile/impact arrives, and second-enemy animations should not all start at the same instant. Check that target warnings are clear, HP changes are apparent, animations are fluid and do not conceal the battlefield, and text/controls never overlap or clip. Watch for distinct attack preparation, Spark projectile, Sigil glyph, Ward shield and movement along the previewed path; verify that characters react and the art still reads at real iPhone size. Inspect a victory or defeat and the Replay Skirmish button.
