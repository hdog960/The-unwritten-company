# THE UNWRITTEN COMPANY — DESIGN BIBLE

**Authority:** Creative vision, narrative logic, playable game systems. All targets below are PLANNED unless supported by the release ledger.

## Identity
Every adventure becomes a legend. Most legends are accidents.
The player is captain of a modest adventuring company in an original fantasy world. No prophecy requiring them to be the chosen hero. The game combines approachable tabletop-style roleplaying and strategically rich battles, with the repeatable high-variance loop of a roguelike. Narrative outcomes must be supported by actual state changes, not random interchangeable text. Comedy grows from characters and consequences, balanced with poignant and frightening moments.

Not a Baldur's Gate 3 port, nor a D&D system clone. Use original setting, characters, classes, rules, dialogue, visual content, mechanics and UI. A d20-inspired probability presentation is permitted; no protected lore or assets.

## World: The Borderlands of Veyr
The Unwritten Company lives at **Inkfall House**, a ramshackle guildhall around an old printing press in **Bellwether**. Veyr is held together by unreliable witness-books and generations of contested chronicles. The company's Chronicle collects the player’s separate legends; rare echoes may reference earlier runs without turning the setting into a contradictory persistent timeline.

Six planned regions:
- **Foxglove March**: orchard villages, petty aristocrats, talking hedges and hereditary curses.
- **Brineward Coast**: trade fleets, storm shrines, black-market cartographers.
- **Glassfall Basin**: subterranean cities, crystal blight and risky scholarship.
- **Tallowfen**: drowned roads, candle rituals, old bargains and hags.
- **Ashen Crownlands**: rival dynasties, fortresses, military orders and conscription.
- **Starling Wilds**: impossible forests, territorial monsters, wandering enclaves and fae agreements.

Factions: Order of the Lantern (protection and zeal), Gilt Cartel (commerce and smuggling), Thorn Assembly (forest communities), Collegium of Unsealed Works (knowledge and risk), Court of Nine Pennants (fractured nobility), Quiet Choir (pilgrims and secrets). Their agendas, power, territory and leader survival evolve within each campaign.

## Hero and party
Create exactly one protagonist: appearance, ancestry, origin, beginning vocation and optional pronouns. Three companion slots are filled through campaign encounters; the player controls all four heroes tactically. If fewer than four are present, difficulty and recruitment opportunity must remain fair. Initial vocations: Vanguard, Duelist, Trailwarden, Shade, Emberwright, Stormcaller, Oathbinder and Cantor; each offers multiple builds and no mandatory rigid party role.

A recurring authored companion ensemble, first twelve targets:
- **Brunna, Disgraced Oathkeeper:** armored defender, worries her abandoned holy order was telling the truth.
- **Kess, Charming Disaster:** elven rogue indebted to an organized criminal network, a habitual liar with unexpected loyalty.
- **Mirelle, Forbidden Scholar:** arcane controller committed to understanding dangerous magic rather than suppressing it.
- **Grit, Reluctant Hero:** goblin chef/trapper who wants a peaceful life and keeps saving people by accident.
- **Veyna, Tidebound Exile:** coastal ritualist, displaced by a political disaster.
- **Orren, Map Eater:** absentminded archivist, excellent at wards but terrible at caution.
- **Tamsin, Last Bellkeeper:** cantor healer haunted by an impossible ringing.
- **Sable, Debt Collector:** masked duelist, strict contractual honor.
- **Harrow, Orchard Witch:** familiar-led hexweaver protective of poor villages.
- **Rook, Disowned Banner:** mercenary breaker, chooses family over employers.
- **Ily, Lantern Thief:** stealthy courier who steals magical light.
- **Nix, Patient Beastkeeper:** monster diplomat and woodland tracker.

Each companion must have original visual identity, tactical kit, goal, moral boundary, secret, faction links and relevant authored dialogue. Recurring across campaigns but stats, items and trust reset. Small optional Chronicle echo callbacks are okay if never contradictory. Party combinations open routes, alter conflicts and unlock banter; no universal requirement to recruit specific companions.

## Procedural campaign director: the signature feature
Target 60–90 minutes per entire campaign, with mid-battle pause/save. Three acts: Hook → Complications/Turning Point → Consequences/resolution. A campaign is a seeded contract, not a randomly regenerated script. At creation lock region, core conflict, actual antagonist and motivation, required clues, critical character identities, faction agendas, pressure system, Turning Point, possible endings and compatible side contracts. The generator uses finite authored story templates with tags, explicit preconditions/effects and fallbacks, not a live paid AI service.

A valid campaign has a reachable beginning, middle, conclusion and at least two distinct routes to every critical fact. Crucial clues remain accessible after a failed persuasion check or an NPC’s death. A seeded decision may create irreversible costs, but no invisible arbitrary softlock or retroactive reveal. Story pressure advances with major choices, not arbitrary real-time waiting. A Turning Point significantly shifts the objective and must be foreshadowed by preexisting evidence. All resolutions honor the already chosen truth package.

Eight candidate premise families: The Weeping Kingdom (crystal plague), The Hollow Crown (missing monarch), The Plague Below (forbidden mine), The Crimson War (fractured armies), A Dragon's Ransom (protected brood), The Bell Without a Tower (lost pilgrimage), A Feast for the Dead (necromantic harvest), The Orchard That Remembers (ancestral bargain).
A premise can hide different culprits only if its clues, objectives, ending and encounters are meaningfully different in each version. No shallow noun-swapping masquerading as variety. Verify broad sample seeds for coherent branching and novelty.

## Accessible choice and chance
Five readable attributes: Might, Agility, Insight, Will, Presence. Show what a choice attempts, what is at stake, a clear estimated success chance and optionally full roll math. Resolve d20-inspired checks reproducibly from campaign seed. Failure usually progresses the fiction by adding an obstacle, risk, price or alternate path. High-risk failures and permadeath are fair if clearly signposted. Companions respond to meaningful decisions; not trivial random approval spam.

## Turn-based tactical rules
Square-grid compact handcrafted arenas, approximately 8x6 initial target subject to viewport testing. Four controllable adventurers, visible initiative order, two action points (AP) plus movement budget per unit turn, 1 AP routine powers / 2 AP major skills, bounded reactions. Preview legality, path, chances, cover, attack range and area effects before commit. Environmental interactions: cover/elevation, water, oil, ignition, frost, lightning conduction, breakables, pushing and ledges. Danger telegraphs are visibly distinct. Enemy families have recognizable visual shapes and strategic identities, not recolors; bosses must have counterplay and narrative relevance. Fight, negotiate, sneak, flee or submit when campaign conditions logically permit.

## Builds, legacy and guild
Within each run: levels, equipment, loot, talents, camp decisions, companion progression and powerful Adventure Boons that combine surprisingly (electric terrain, ember chain reactions, ally protection, etc.). Aim for exciting combinations without infinite loops or mandatory meta upgrades.
Between runs: Inkfall House guild improvements and Chronicle entries; permanent progress is mostly horizontal (new origins, companion possibilities, contracts, item pools, cosmetics and story modules), not massive permanent damage inflation. Chronicle records party, seed, region, truth, choices, endings, friends and enemies; rare optional echoes never retcon current seed.

## Art and sound
Art target named **The Illuminated Table**: richly painted ink contours, expressive illustrated portraits, real tactile parchment/brass/leather and animated miniature-diorama figures. Distinctive palette per region; warm ember-and-ink hub. Storybook charm with dark unsettling edges. Clear battle silhouettes and readable VFX. Original/licensed audio only; sound never required to understand actions.

## Platform, pricing and scope
iPhone landscape Safari and installed Home Screen PWA; static GitHub Pages, no required paid APIs or external services. Offline-capable, versioned local saves and export/import. Browser storage is not a cloud save. Free playable game; no pay-to-win and monetization is outside v1's initial obligations. Real App Store publication is separate from website distribution. Original creative IP everywhere.

## Definition of complete
Art judged by the player at Gate A and beyond; tactical play enjoyable; several fully authored seed-different campaigns with actual conclusions; meaningful party interactions; Chronicle and guild progression; persistent saves and accessible mobile control; tested, deployed, truthfully documented. Never fake content or call a menu a completed campaign.
